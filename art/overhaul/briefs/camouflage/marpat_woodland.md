## Pattern and material

Woodland digital family: small square/stepped pixels in black, dark green, olive and brown over a lighter green base. Use clustered blocks of different density, not random confetti. Do not copy USMC pixel arrangement or insignia.

Use a matte woven field uniform material. Keep folds and seam shadows in the garment mesh/material response, not painted as fake garment geometry. The camouflage variant changes fabric only; use the selected jacket/trousers cut from its apparel brief, with no new armor or load-bearing design.

## Scale, UV and pose review

Small pixel groups; keep block edges crisp but filtered enough to avoid shimmer. UV alignment may rotate panels but should not create obvious mirrored repeats. Make separate panel UVs follow the fabric direction where directional, but do not mirror or reuse a recognizable cluster. Continue color density across shoulder, cuff, crotch, knee and pocket seams. In crouch, kneel, shoulder raise and first-person sleeve bends, the pattern must deform with fabric rather than smear, bunch into a hard seam, or disappear under filtering. Review a close crop, a full garment, and the normal gameplay distance; keep LOD pattern frequency stable and avoid moiré.
