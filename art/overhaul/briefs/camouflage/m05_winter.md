## Pattern and material

Finnish M05 cold-weather candidate, distinct from the snow-white variant: gray-green/olive and muted brown breakup on a cooler medium field as visible in the active references. Do not borrow the white/dark pattern from M05 Snow.

Use a matte woven field uniform material. Keep folds and seam shadows in the garment mesh/material response, not painted as fake garment geometry. The camouflage variant changes fabric only; use the selected jacket/trousers cut from its apparel brief, with no new armor or load-bearing design.

## Scale, UV and pose review

Medium irregular pixel/leaf clusters; set scale for a cold-weather garment and maintain continuity across folds and layered panels. Make separate panel UVs follow the fabric direction where directional, but do not mirror or reuse a recognizable cluster. Continue color density across shoulder, cuff, crotch, knee and pocket seams. In crouch, kneel, shoulder raise and first-person sleeve bends, the pattern must deform with fabric rather than smear, bunch into a hard seam, or disappear under filtering. Review a close crop, a full garment, and the normal gameplay distance; keep LOD pattern frequency stable and avoid moiré.
