# multicam_alpine — reference boundary and snow-pattern direction

The active five-file set and source roles are recorded in [the local index](</Users/vladimir/Downloads/RAID_GAME_Art_Overhaul_References/camouflage/multicam_alpine/sources.json>). The duplicate PATRIOT LITE sample was moved to the quarantine folder; it does not count toward the five references.

## What the sources establish

- MultiCam's official [Patterns page](https://multicampattern.com/patterns/) describes Alpine as a pattern for snow-covered operating areas and provides the official image now indexed as reference 06. That describes the product's stated environmental role; it does not establish military adoption, service status, or a license for our use.
- Three distinct visual types are represented: textile swatches (01, 04), a matched winter oversuit shown on a mannequin/model (03, 05), and an official brand snow-pattern image (06). The two suits help assess body-scale coverage; product photography has no reliable evidence of issue or current military use.
- The set's exact military-use status remains unconfirmed in `modern-use-evidence.md`. A secondary attribution of an official U.S. Army 10th Special Forces photo to MultiCam Alpine was not promoted into the counted source set because the primary DVIDS caption does not identify the pattern.

## Pattern design and legal boundary

Use the references to study snow-value structure: a predominantly off-white field, pale gray areas, and irregular soft-edged mid-size forms that read as broader masses from distance while retaining small transitions up close. The printable texture should scale across sleeves, torso and trousers without obvious mirrored seams; avoid high-frequency digital noise and avoid shifting to a high-contrast black/white split.

These are cues for an original RAID GAME snow camouflage, not instructions to trace the branded pattern. The official source is copyrighted and the references do not grant rights to reproduce MultiCam Alpine. Unless specific permission is documented, make a distinct snow pattern with its own palette and cluster topology and do not ship the exact name/logo or a direct copy of the official swatch as runtime art.

## Production checks after art/legal approval

- Ensure the original tile is seamless and test value grouping against snow in shade, open daylight, and mixed vegetation at near/mid/far distance.
- Check UV scale and continuity over shoulder/elbow/knee folds and at LOD changes. A plain white outer shell may be a useful separate gameplay choice but should not be silently conflated with this patterned variant.
- Keep the brand's 06 image reference-only. The commercial listing images are not clean runtime source textures or evidence of authorization.
