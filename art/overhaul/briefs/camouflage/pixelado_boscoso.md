## Pattern and material
Create an original compact woodland digital family: deep moss/olive ground, medium green and dark forest clusters, sparse warm brown and small khaki breaks. Use stepped pixel edges grouped into uneven macro blocks; avoid a uniform checkerboard and avoid tracing the identifiable M09 repeat. Keep clusters roughly hand-sized on the garment with smaller pixel transitions close up, stable over sleeve/trouser seams and legible at normal gameplay distance.

## Scale, UV and pose review

Small stepped pixels arranged in medium clusters; maintain enough value contrast for gameplay read without turning into checkerboard. Make separate panel UVs follow the fabric direction where directional, but do not mirror or reuse a recognizable cluster. Continue color density across shoulder, cuff, crotch, knee and pocket seams. In crouch, kneel, shoulder raise and first-person sleeve bends, the pattern must deform with fabric rather than smear, bunch into a hard seam, or disappear under filtering. Review a close crop, a full garment, and the normal gameplay distance; keep LOD pattern frequency stable and avoid moiré.
