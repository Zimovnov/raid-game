# digital_desert — camouflage art brief

**Reference status:** six relevant sources: five independent textile/print swatches plus a sixth full front view of a commercial desert-digital BDU. The target is an original desert digital family; the swatches are material/pixel-scale archetypes, not an exact service pattern. See [`sources.json`](/Users/vladimir/Downloads/RAID_GAME_Art_Overhaul_References/camouflage/digital_desert/sources.json).

## Pattern and material

Compose a warm sand/tan base with muted umber and pale khaki pixel blocks, deliberately uneven in scale and density. Keep larger stepped groupings readable at gameplay distance and smaller grain subordinate; do not reproduce a national repeat or copy the commercial sample. Use matte cotton/nyco ripstop visual response, with subdued weave texture and seam/fold shading.

## Scale, UV and pose review

Use varied pixel groups rather than a uniform checker. Avoid mirroring notable clusters and keep transitions believable across pocket, knee, elbow and shoulder panels. Review print close-up, full uniform and crouched/aiming silhouettes; preserve readable color groups through LOD and avoid shimmer.

## Provenance and claims

All images are reference-only. The garment image supports clothing-scale behavior and not a claim of military adoption or rights to its specific print. No third-party labels or source pixels in runtime assets.
