## Pattern and material

Build an original muted green/olive woodland composition with broad, irregular horizontal-to-diagonal brush islands in forest green, dark olive, subdued tan and earthy brown. Preserve soft-edged streaks that break into smaller lobes, with broad negative-space ground visible between groups; avoid bright lime, tiny digital pixels, long clean tiger stripes, and large leaf silhouettes. The design should read as a dense broad-brush woodland family at garment scale while using newly authored shapes and colors, not a reconstructed Flora repeat.

The active refs have distinct roles: 01 is a fabric/button macro for weave and value breakup; 02 shows shoulder and sleeve-pocket seams; 04 shows a folded field-jacket panel; 05 gives only dark field-scale silhouette context; 06 is a full jacket/trouser set for broad panel repeat scale. The seller/source identity is not proof of a standard or issue status, and #05 lighting is not a palette authority.

Use matte woven field cloth. Apply camouflage as a fabric variant to the selected jacket/trouser cuts; it does not define jacket construction, insignia, armor or load-bearing equipment.

## Scale, UV and motion

Keep broad marks visible across torso and sleeve at normal viewing distance, with medium lobes that do not collapse into flat noise at LOD1. Continue the visual density across sleeve, pocket, placket, thigh and knee seams without creating a continuous stripe at UV boundaries. Author an original seamless map; avoid mirrored cluster pairs. Check close texture, full-body silhouette, arm raise, crouch and kneel for scale, stretch, moiré and abrupt pattern discontinuity.
