## Pattern and material

The No.4 reference is a green/olive base with irregular dark green, brown and tan square-pixel groups; it is not the blue-gray MAF digital uniform mistakenly represented by a prior active ref. Make a distinct original forest digital pattern with uneven block clusters at several scales and a subdued green-brown palette; do not trace pixels, insignia, flags or garment labels.

Use matte woven uniform cloth. Pattern is a fabric variant only; jacket and trouser shape stays governed by its apparel brief. Mixed-force 16 July photographs are secondary sources for fabric folds and worn scale, not attribution of every visible uniform to SAF.

## Scale, UV and pose review

Keep pixel edges stepped but not grid-regular; preserve broad color groups at distance and avoid shimmer at LOD. Do not mirror distinctive cluster layouts. Check transitions over shoulder, elbow, knee and pocket seams as the wearer crouches, kneels and shoulders a weapon; ensure folds deform the print rather than smear it.

## Provenance and claims

Use current MINDEF photos only as visual references, not runtime content. Their captions establish No.4 uniform use; the image match to the candidate pattern is visual evidence, not a license or claim of exact pixel identity.
