## Pattern and material

Observed palette is pale warm sand/stone with irregular rust-brown stepped digital islands and scattered small marks. Preserve its pixel-grid character at multiple scales while designing an independent game pattern; do not trace the historical repeat, insignia or uniforms. Use a matte woven ripstop response. Garment construction follows the separate apparel briefs.

## Scale, UV and pose review

Keep islands legible at gameplay distance and smaller pixel breakup subordinate. Avoid mirrored repeat fragments and abrupt seams over shoulders, elbows, knees and pockets. Test print scale in close-up, full garment, bright arid terrain and shaded interiors; check crouch, kneel and first-person aiming for UV smear and LOD shimmer.
