## Scale, UV and pose review

Make separate panel UVs follow the fabric direction where directional, but do not mirror or reuse a recognizable cluster. Continue color density across shoulder, cuff, crotch, knee and pocket seams. Review a close crop, a full garment, and the normal gameplay distance; keep LOD pattern frequency stable and avoid moiré.
