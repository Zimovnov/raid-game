## Pattern and material

Partizan woodland: dark olive, green, brown and muted tan with broad irregular vertical/diagonal swaths and small breakup marks. Keep a dense shaded woodland read.

Use a matte woven field uniform material. Keep folds and seam shadows in the garment mesh/material response, not painted as fake garment geometry. The camouflage variant changes fabric only; use the selected jacket/trousers cut from its apparel brief, with no new armor or load-bearing design.

## Scale, UV and pose review

Large clusters cross the torso and sleeves; avoid duplicated patches or a single central shape that reads as a logo. Make separate panel UVs follow the fabric direction where directional, but do not mirror or reuse a recognizable cluster. Continue color density across shoulder, cuff, crotch, knee and pocket seams. In crouch, kneel, shoulder raise and first-person sleeve bends, the pattern must deform with fabric rather than smear, bunch into a hard seam, or disappear under filtering. Review a close crop, a full garment, and the normal gameplay distance; keep LOD pattern frequency stable and avoid moiré.
