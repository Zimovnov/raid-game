## Pattern and material

Build an original tan/khaki field pattern with muted green, brown and dark flecks. Combine soft-edged, overlapping organic marks at fine, medium and large scales; avoid copying MultiCam/OCP cluster shapes or exact color placement. Use the full uniform image for garment-scale value grouping and product references for fabric detail only.

Use matte woven ripstop; color/pattern variant changes fabric only, while jacket and trouser construction comes from their separate apparel briefs. Keep seams, folds and pockets in geometry/material response, not painted garment details.

## Scale, UV and pose review

Maintain readable medium-scale clusters across shoulder, cuff, crotch, knee and cargo pockets. Avoid mirrored recognizable clusters and hard discontinuities at seams. Check macro close-up, full garment and normal gameplay distance under crouch, kneel, shoulder raise and first-person sleeve bends; filter to avoid moiré while preserving color breakup.

## Provenance and claims

Reference photographs are for visual study only. Do not include source logos, insignia, labels or source pixels in runtime textures. Army current-uniform guidance supports OCP use; it does not grant rights to the exact repeat.
