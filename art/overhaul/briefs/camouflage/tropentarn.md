# 3-Farb-Flecktarn — camouflage art brief (`tropentarn` project ID)

## Naming and evidence limits

The project ID `tropentarn` is retained, but the target is the three-color Bundeswehr deployment print commonly described as **3-Farb-Flecktarn**. The word “Tropentarn” is ambiguous and can also refer to lightweight tropical-climate Flecktarn clothing; do not merge that clothing weight with the three-color print. The Bundeswehr uniform overview identifies a three-color deployment print and the 2018 Guardian Angels report identifies the deployed print historically. The currently sold Leo Köhler combat pant provides present-day **commercial product** evidence for garment availability only; it does not prove current Bundeswehr issue. No claim of current military issue is made.

This is an original game interpretation, not a reproduction of an official pattern. Do not use source pixels, insignia, logos, or exact repeat placement.

Three current product images replace them with distinct views. Commercial availability and historical Bundeswehr imagery are separate evidence classes.

## Original art direction

Use a muted warm sand/khaki ground with medium earth-brown and dark olive flecks. Distribute irregular rounded clusters and islands at small-to-medium scale, with a few larger connected blotches; avoid a repeated dot grid or uniform scatter. Keep the overall three-value relationship readable but do not copy a measured official ratio, exact contours, or swatch palette. Apply subtle matte woven cloth response, not plastic gloss or hard outlines.

## UV, garment and movement

Apply the print as a fabric-only variant on the jacket and trousers selected by their separate apparel briefs. Keep apparent scale consistent between torso, sleeves, thighs, knees, and pocket flaps. Offset and rotate panels to avoid cloned clusters and obvious mirroring; maintain color density across seams without aligning distinctive blobs. Review close crop, full garment and gameplay distance during standing, crouch, kneel, shoulder raise, elbow and knee bends; avoid smeared marks, visible seam jumps and LOD moiré. Armor, helmet, webbing and backpacks remain outside this camouflage rework except fit defects.
