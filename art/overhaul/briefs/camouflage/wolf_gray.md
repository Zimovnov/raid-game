## Material and garment use

Use a muted cool-neutral medium gray with restrained value variation, matte woven ripstop on uniform panels and compatible slightly stretchier torso fabric only where the selected apparel cut calls for it. Do not add a pattern. The references establish color and garment-scale response, not exact material composition for the game.

## Scale, UV and pose review

Keep weave subtle enough to avoid noise at gameplay distance. No mirrored print issue applies; preserve fabric direction where the weave is visible. Check elbow/knee folds and seam shading in crouch, kneel and first-person aiming.
