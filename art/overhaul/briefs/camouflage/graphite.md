## Material and garment use

Use a charcoal-neutral gray with a matte woven surface. Preserve separation between panels through roughness and low-contrast value shifts, not black painted outlines. The Red Kap work shirt is a scale/cloth proxy only; construct the actual silhouette from the appropriate apparel brief. Do not add tactical features based on the workwear proxy.

## Scale, UV and pose review

Keep the weave visible only at close range. Preserve cloth direction along sleeves and legs; test shading at folds and seams under aiming, crouch and kneel poses. Avoid crushing the material into featureless black in low light.
