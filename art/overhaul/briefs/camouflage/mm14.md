**Reference status:** a current Ministry of Defence of Ukraine image dated 7 April 2026 shows MM-14 on a plate carrier; it is evidence of current equipment use and pixel scale, not proof of a uniform standard. The active set includes original MM-14 uniform/product imagery and replaces the mismatched M25 candidate. ## Pattern and material

Use a restrained green/olive/brown pixel family with stepped clusters and varied block scale, observed in MM-14 references. Compose an independently designed repeat rather than tracing pixel locations, insignia or a protected national pattern. The official current image is a plate-carrier close-up, so use it for pattern color/scale only; garment construction comes from the apparel briefs.

Use matte woven fabric. The pattern changes fabric only, with no armor or load-bearing redesign in this camouflage task.

## Scale, UV and pose review

Keep block groups legible at full-body distance and reduce high-frequency shimmer. Preserve print continuity around shoulder, elbow, knee and pocket panels without mirroring recognizable groups. Test close print, full uniform, normal gameplay distance and folded cloth in crouch/kneel/aiming poses.

## Provenance and claims

Reference photos establish visual observations only. Current-use evidence is equipment-specific; do not claim that the shown image proves MM-14 is the sole or universal uniform standard. Do not copy source imagery, logos, insignia or labels into runtime textures.
