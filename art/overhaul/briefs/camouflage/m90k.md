## Pattern and material

Target an original arid angular-splinter family: pale sand and khaki base, broad interlocking taupe/olive-gray and rust-brown angular fields, with sparse darker olive-brown accents. Keep the abrupt polygonal edges and large-to-medium islands distinct from the green woodland M90 family, but invent a new shape map and color values. Do not trace the photographed arrangement or use the TAC-UP product as an exact-texture source.

The active references include front, rear, and crouched field-trouser views, plus a worn boonie and side-worn field cap. They show the current commercial color/pattern behavior across garment folds and small panels. The trousers show cargo-pocket flaps, gathered ankle cuffs, front seat and knee compression; use them only as material/scale guidance. Hat cuts and attached webbing are separate apparel topics, not part of this camouflage brief.

Use a matte woven field fabric. Keep the pattern fabric-only: apply it to the selected existing jacket/trouser apparel cut, with no implied new armor or load-bearing design.

## Scale, UV and pose review

Use large, irregular angular blocks at the cloth repeat scale; keep each island readable on the cap panel, trouser pocket, knee, and lower leg without producing stripes across seams. Split UVs along garment seams, keep the same authored repeat scale across clothing items, and avoid mirroring recognizable clusters. Verify front-to-rear continuity, crouch/kneel distortion, first-person sleeve scale, distance filtering, moiré, and LOD transitions.

## Provenance and claims

Current commercial availability is documented by TAC-UP GEAR product pages for its [field trousers](https://tacupgear.com/clothing/field-trousers-m90k-desert), [boonie](https://tacupgear.com/hats/boonie-hat-m90k-desert), and [field cap](https://tacupgear.com/hats/cap-m90k-desert). This supports a current product-market example only; it does not establish military adoption, a Swedish government specification, or unrestricted rights. Use an original game label and original texture; do not include source photos, product labels, insignia, or copied pixels in runtime.
