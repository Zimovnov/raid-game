## Pattern and material

Use an original forest repeat inspired by the observed Finnish M05 palette: muted light and dark greens, warm brown and charcoal accents in medium-to-large irregular soft-edged clusters with fine breakup. Do not trace the protected repeat or badges. Keep matte woven ripstop response; garment shape remains controlled by the separate apparel briefs.

## Scale, UV and pose review

Maintain broad forest value groups at distance and finer breakup up close. Do not mirror distinctive clusters; continue the pattern logically across sleeves, elbows, knees, pockets and seam joins. Check crouch, kneel, shoulder raise and first-person bends for pattern smear or moiré.

## Provenance and claims

The images show current retail M05 products and historical pattern use, not a verified statement that all Finnish forces currently use this exact uniform. Use a neutral in-game label and no third-party imagery or symbols at runtime.
