# cadpat_mt — reference boundary and original-game direction

Reference images are in [the local source index](</Users/vladimir/Downloads/RAID_GAME_Art_Overhaul_References/camouflage/cadpat_mt/sources.json>). They are research material, not runtime assets. Four field photos are U.S. Army public-domain works on their Commons file records; that does not clear the CADPAT pattern itself for commercial reproduction.

## What the verified material supports

- The Canadian Army says CADPAT (MT) is a five-colour, brown-and-green, mid-spectrum pattern intended to replace TW and AR, and said in 2024 that it was being phased in over two years. That confirms the Canadian Army's issuing/transition statement at that date; a completed transition or present 2026 unit-level issue rate is not established by these sources. See [the official 2024 Army communication](https://www.canada.ca/en/army/services/for-the-soldier/comm-strategy-2024-2026.html), [the 2024 uniform notice](https://www.canada.ca/en/army/news-publications/2024/02/cadpat-mt-uniforms.html), and the project's [`modern-use-evidence.md`](modern-use-evidence.md).
- Photos 03 and 04 show CADPAT MT on Canadian soldiers in a snowfield at two distinct distances/compositions; 05 gives a closer crouched view in a woodland setting; 06 is a separate 2025 Arctic Forge driver-training group. Together use these to study broad value grouping, pixel clusters across sleeves/trousers/creases, and contrast at full-body versus close distance. The two Arctic Forge frames are separate captures from one event, not independent evidence of adoption.
- Photo 02 is a field-use image attributed to CADPAT MT by a secondary page; its greener cast and partially obscured uniform make it a lower-confidence appearance reference. The previously included commercial fabric listing has been quarantined because its CADPAT MT label was unverified.

## Runtime material must be original

DND's official [CADPAT licensing policy](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/corporate/intellectual-property/crown-copyright/cadpat.html) says commercial requests to use CADPAT are controlled and may be denied. Do not trace the source textile, reproduce its exact cluster map, or retain recognizable Canadian maple-leaf shapes. Keep this as an art-direction target only unless separate written rights clearance is documented.

For a separately approved original RAID GAME woodland multi-terrain pattern, use a distinct five-value brown/olive family with softened luminance transitions and irregular small-to-medium clusters across a broad cloth repeat. Avoid exact CADPAT colour samples, pixel-cluster topology, recognizable leaf motifs, and the source's repeat map; produce new shape grammar and palette from scratch. The intention is general concealment across mixed vegetation/earth, not an assertion that RAID GAME's original design is CADPAT or officially adopted.

## Production checks once legal/art gate is resolved

- Build a new seamless tile, never a direct crop, overlay, trace, or color-shift of these refs. Establish repeat scale against the existing character UV texel density; verify sleeve-to-torso and trouser continuity without mirrored tells.
- Test at near, mid, and far view: clusters should merge into broad value masses at distance rather than become high-frequency noise; inspect seams, folds, knees, elbows, and compression at LOD transitions.
- Store separate albedo/palette variants only where the project material pipeline supports them; do not change geometry or introduce Canadian insignia. Treat visible clothing construction in 03–05 as a camouflage appearance cue, not a garment-model brief.
- Before any runtime texture is created, confirm legal review and art-direction approval. CADPAT itself is excluded from production absent specific permission.

## Evidence limits

The active set contains five distinct files, including four official U.S. Army field photographs and one lower-confidence secondary field image; it has been visually reviewed. The field images provide useful worn scale and folds, though two are from one exercise and photo 02 remains lower confidence. No right to reproduce CADPAT is implied. Current transition status after the 2024 phase-in statement remains unverified in this evidence set.
