# woodland — camouflage art brief

**Reference status:** five distinct, visually reviewed images are listed in [`sources.json`](/Users/vladimir/Downloads/RAID_GAME_Art_Overhaul_References/camouflage/woodland/sources.json). All are historical/commercial M81 garment references and are strictly reference-only; they do not establish a right to reproduce the protected repeat or a current issue claim. The game material is an original generic woodland composition.

## Target composition

Build a broad-form temperate woodland family using four interlocking, irregular masses: muted leaf green, olive, warm earth-brown and a restrained very-dark green-brown. Use a few small pale khaki breaks only where they improve separation. Forms should have soft organic lobes and occasional tapered branches, with clear large/medium/small scale hierarchy; avoid the exact M81 silhouettes, placement, frequency, and black/tan balance. Do not label it M81 or imply adoption by a real force.

## Fabric and garment application

Use a matte woven field-uniform surface with low, fine weave response, slightly softened printed edges and subtle value variation from wear. The fabric variant changes print only; the selected jacket/trouser cut comes from its apparel brief. Do not add armor/load-bearing details. On jackets, carry the pattern consistently over collar, shoulder, sleeves, pocket flaps and cuffs; on trousers, continue it over seat, cargo pockets, knees and lower legs. Do not align identical shapes across seams or create mirrored repeats.

## Scale, UV and movement

Target visually broad patches at torso and thigh scale, medium forms across sleeves and cargo pockets, and only occasional small connectors. UV islands may be rotated or offset to avoid recognizable repetition, but keep apparent print scale stable between panels and LODs. Check fabric at close range, full-body range and normal gameplay distance, including raised arms, crouch, kneel and first-person sleeve bends. Pattern must deform with the cloth without stretching into narrow streaks, creating conspicuous seam breaks, or aliasing into moiré.
