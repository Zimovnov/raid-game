## Pattern and material

Create an original winter overlayer pattern with an off-white base, sparse cool gray and charcoal patches, and irregular breaks that remain distinct against snow-covered trees. Combine large low-frequency breakup with small soft-edged flecks; avoid copying M05 Snow or any reference repeat. Use a matte low-sheen shell with slightly crinkled lightweight-overgarment response; the garment should read as an outer camouflage layer.

## Scale, UV and pose review

Preserve broad pale fields and dark silhouette-breaking marks at distance. Do not mirror recognizable clusters; keep transition behavior across hood, shoulders, elbows and knees plausible. Check folds over the base clothing, crouch and aiming pose, snow daylight and shaded forest; prevent detail shimmer and avoid the texture dissolving into uniform white.

## Provenance and claims

The references show distinct commercial or field examples; they do not establish one current national standard or authorize copying. Use a neutral in-game name and no source imagery, labels or insignia at runtime.
