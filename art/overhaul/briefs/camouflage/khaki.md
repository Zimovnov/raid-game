## Material and garment use

Use a neutral tan-khaki base with restrained woven variation and matte response. Do not add a pattern. The reference shirt supports color, pocket scale and fabric folds only; use the separate apparel brief for the selected in-game garment cut.

## Scale and lighting

Keep weave subtle at gameplay distance and prevent the fabric from clipping to flat yellow or brown in bright and shaded lighting. Review seam/fold highlights during crouch, kneel and first-person aiming.
