## Pattern and material
AMCU art direction is a distinct green-dominant Australian woodland mixture: warm olive/sage base, mid green/brown fragments, sparse cream-gray breaks. Use nested irregular islands, not a regular digital grid and not a generic Multicam copy. Pattern scale should read as medium cloth-sized clusters on a full torso/leg while retaining smaller interlocking fragments close up. References document garment/fabric context; the runtime repeat must be original.

## Scale, UV and pose review

Mixed small/medium fragments; no tile blocks. Keep scale stable from torso to leg, distort naturally with pose. Make separate panel UVs follow the fabric direction where directional, but do not mirror or reuse a recognizable cluster. Continue color density across shoulder, cuff, crotch, knee and pocket seams. In crouch, kneel, shoulder raise and first-person sleeve bends, the pattern must deform with fabric rather than smear, bunch into a hard seam, or disappear under filtering. Review a close crop, a full garment, and the normal gameplay distance; keep LOD pattern frequency stable and avoid moiré.
