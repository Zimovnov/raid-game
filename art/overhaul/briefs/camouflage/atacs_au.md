## Pattern and material
A-TACS AU pattern is a warm dry-ground family: pale sand and stone base, mid-tone ochre/taupe islands, small olive-gray shadows, organic digital fragments that form rounded macro islands. Keep irregular cloud-like clusters at mixed scales; no even square grid. Use the official swatch for observed color relationships and field images only for body-scale contrast. Runtime pattern must be independently composed; do not trace the proprietary repeat.

## Scale, UV and pose review

Fine-to-medium microtexture; ensure larger value groups survive low-poly distance while small fragments do not alias. Make separate panel UVs follow the fabric direction where directional, but do not mirror or reuse a recognizable cluster. Continue color density across shoulder, cuff, crotch, knee and pocket seams. In crouch, kneel, shoulder raise and first-person sleeve bends, the pattern must deform with fabric rather than smear, bunch into a hard seam, or disappear under filtering. Review a close crop, a full garment, and the normal gameplay distance; keep LOD pattern frequency stable and avoid moiré.

## Provenance and claims

Do not include source logos, flags, insignia, screenshots, product labels, or third-party pixels in runtime textures. Reference photographs establish visual observations only; a source image or product listing does not establish military adoption, rights to reproduce a pattern, or the final garment cut. Where the current-use review says “not confirmed,” preserve that uncertainty and use an original neutral in-game label.
