## Material and garment use

Use a deep blue-black/navy field fabric with subtle matte cotton/ripstop/drill weave. Keep hue readable in low light without turning it black. The Mil-Tec and Moko shirts show solid color and garment folds only; the actual game silhouette remains in the apparel brief.

## Scale and lighting

Keep weave low-contrast at gameplay distance. Separate panels with roughness and fold response rather than outlines; test in bright and dim scenes plus first-person sleeve bending.
