## Silhouette and construction

Create a loose, lightweight field oversmock ending around the upper thigh, worn over a base layer. Keep a roomy hood with a face-covering veil/mesh option attached to the hood silhouette, a high neck, a central front closure partly concealed by the placket, a large lower-front pouch area, gathered or adjustable waist, loose sleeves and cuff adjustment. Back view is comparatively plain with the hood and shoulder seams defining the silhouette; do not invent back pockets or armor. Treat the separate tan outer-shell image as a color/material/cut variant reference, not proof that every Sumrak model has identical panels.

## Material, color and detail

Use matte lightweight woven cloth with soft folds and a subdued fabric response; avoid the stiffness of a heavy combat jacket. Keep the game garment's chosen camouflage as a separate fabric material and avoid reproducing the SSO leaf repeat or any watermark. Use restrained seam, drawcord and closure detail at low-poly scale. No logos, patches, flags or source-specific branding.

## 13-joint fit and review

Keep the oversmock anchored to the existing 13-joint apparel contract. Check the hood/neck and torso hem during head turns, shoulders/upper arms during raised-arm poses, sleeve volume at elbow bends, and front pouch/hem during crouch and kneel. Cloth should not clip through the face-cover or duplicate the base torso layer. First-person sleeves use the same garment identity/texture scale as third-person sleeves. Review front, rear, three-quarter, hood-up and lighter-shell views before approval of a target model; LOD0 and LOD1 must preserve readable silhouette and closure placement.
