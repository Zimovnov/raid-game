# head_patrol — production brief

**Reference status:** five patrol/fatigue-cap product images were visually inspected in [`sources.json`](/Users/vladimir/Downloads/RAID_GAME_Art_Overhaul_References/apparel/head_patrol/sources.json). They show low-profile crown, short visor, front/side silhouette, crown seams and one rear adjustment view. Uniform colors, national markings and logos are not design targets; images are reference-only.

## Silhouette and construction

Create a soft low-profile patrol cap with a shallow paneled crown, short gently curved visor, bound lower edge, and a simple rear adjustment tab or elastic insert. Preserve clearance over ears and forehead; keep the crown slightly structured without becoming a hard helmet. Avoid exact military insignia, flag tabs, or service-specific stitching.

## Material, UV and fit

Use matte cotton twill or similar woven cloth in olive, coyote or the selected uniform fabric. Unwrap crown panels as separate pieces with seam-aligned UVs; keep the visor's fabric direction consistent and avoid a visible front-center mirrored seam. Allow mild fabric compression around the head and a firm visor edge. Check neutral, head-turn and crouch poses so the cap does not float, clip into hair or intersect eyewear. LOD0/LOD1 preserve crown and visor silhouette. Back panel details are simplified to the generic adjustment shown by the product reference; verify final fit in runtime.
