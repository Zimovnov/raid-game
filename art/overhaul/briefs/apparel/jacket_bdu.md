**Reference status:** five active images in [`sources.json`](/Users/vladimir/Downloads/RAID_GAME_Art_Overhaul_References/apparel/jacket_bdu/sources.json) were visually reviewed individually: two clean front/product views, one worn field view, one additional full-front view and a new worn rear three-quarter view. ## Silhouette and construction

Use a loose hip-length BDU coat with folded collar, covered center button line, four large front bellows pockets with flaps, chest pen slot where shown, reinforced elbows and button-adjustable cuffs. The newly added rear view confirms a simple shoulder/back panel, sleeve seams and hem without rear pockets; keep that back plain. Do not add ACU-style angled pockets, shoulder storage or modern armor features.

## Material and movement

Use matte woven ripstop or twill with soft, low-poly cloth folds; choose one fabric variant and keep it consistent across front/back. Make pocket flaps and reinforced elbows slightly heavier than the main cloth without looking rigid. Check arm raise, elbow bend, crouch and torso twist for cuff, pocket and hem clipping. UVs follow the panel grain; no mirrored recognizable camouflage shapes or source pixel transfer. LOD0/LOD1 preserve the four-pocket silhouette and collar.

## Limits

The catalog includes different fabric and color variants, so use common BDU construction cues only. Exclude source labels, patches, logos and exact camouflage topology. Product views do not define all military issue variants; the target is an original game garment.
