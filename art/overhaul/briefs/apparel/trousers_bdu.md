## Silhouette and construction

Build a classic straight-leg BDU trouser with waistband, adjustable waist tabs, button or zip fly per selected variant, two hand pockets, two bellowed thigh cargo pockets, reinforced knees, six-pocket overall layout, and drawcord or simple cuff adjustment. New rear/side views clarify seat, rear pockets, cargo-pocket projection and hems over footwear. Do not merge ACU calf-pocket shapes or later knee-pad sleeves into the baseline BDU cut.

## Material and motion

Use matte cotton/poly ripstop or twill depending on chosen variant; distinguish reinforced knees by seam/panel, not by hard armor. Keep camouflage and weave scale continuous across leg panels without mirrored clusters. Test crouch, kneel, sprint and seat bend; cargo pockets should move with the thigh, seat should not tear visually at the pelvis seam, and leg hems should clear the boot. LOD0/LOD1 preserve waist, cargo pockets and reinforced knee shape.

## Limits

The source images include solid and different camouflage variants. They establish a shared BDU cut family, not an exact textile specification or one mandatory button/zip configuration. Choose one coherent variant and omit brands, source labels and logos.
