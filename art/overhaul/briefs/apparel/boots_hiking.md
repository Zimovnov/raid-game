# boots_hiking — production brief

**Reference status:** five product and field photographs were inspected on the apparel contact sheet and individually spot-checked; see [`sources.json`](/Users/vladimir/Downloads/RAID_GAME_Art_Overhaul_References/apparel/boots_hiking/sources.json). The set includes side profiles, a tread/outsole view, worn/environmental context and alternative mid/high ankle forms. Product views repeat one model family, which is useful for panel and sole construction but not five independent boot designs. All images are reference-only.

## Silhouette and construction

Target a mid/high hiking boot with a padded ankle collar, protective toe bumper, lace closure, reinforced heel counter and a thick lugged outsole. Keep a clear ankle shaft and moderately wide foot profile; do not make it a low trail shoe or a smooth dress boot. Use restrained overlay panels and eyelet/loop hardware, with a flexible crease across the instep and a defined outsole sidewall. The boot reference set mixes models; use it for generic hiking footwear construction, not a particular brand copy.

## Material and color

Use muted earth-brown or coyote suede/textile panels with darker rubber outsole and a low-sheen finish. Material boundaries should be legible in low-poly form without excessive contrast or tiny lace hardware. Keep weave/grain subtle at game distance, and exclude all logos and brand markings.

## Fit and movement

Anchor the boot to the existing foot joint with a stable heel/toe silhouette. Check foot roll, toe-off and crouch/kneel contact so the sole does not float, shear or intersect the ground. Keep the tread readable at close view and reduce it at distance; LOD0/LOD1 preserve the ankle collar, toe protection and outsole shape. The photos show static product/worn scenes rather than this game's animation, so motion behavior remains a runtime validation task.
