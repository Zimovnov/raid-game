# trousers_acu — production brief

**Reference status:** six active images include older and current ACU/OCP trouser fronts, an outdoor worn view, an official Propper rear view and a close cargo-pocket construction view; see [`sources.json`](/Users/vladimir/Downloads/RAID_GAME_Art_Overhaul_References/apparel/trousers_acu/sources.json). The former A2CU flight-suit trouser image was removed because it is a different garment. Color/pattern and fabric variants show related ACU cuts, not one interchangeable exact uniform specification. Images are reference-only.

## Silhouette and construction

Use a straight, moderately loose trouser leg with a shaped waistband, belt loops, button fly, forward-tilted thigh cargo pockets, small calf pockets and reinforced seat. The rear reference confirms seat and pocket placement; the close crop clarifies cargo flap, stitching and panel scale. Distinguish the knee from the cargo pocket and keep a clear break at the ankle. Do not add hard knee pads when the selected variant does not show a pad sleeve.

## Material, UV and pose

Use matte ripstop woven cloth with subtle grid texture and the matching uniform camo/solid variant. Place UVs along front/back leg panels and pockets; do not mirror recognizable camouflage clusters or stretch the pattern around the knee. Check standing, crouch, kneel, sprint and seat deformation so the rear panel, cargo pockets and calf pockets remain attached to the cloth and do not intersect. LOD0/LOD1 preserve the waist, pockets and leg silhouette.

## Limits

The sources include multiple ACU/OCP generations and cloth variants, so use them to define a coherent generic ACU silhouette, not to merge every feature. Flight-suit trouser construction is excluded. Exact fabric blend and military specification are not gameplay requirements; no source logo, branding or pattern image may enter runtime.
