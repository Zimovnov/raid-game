# gloves_olive — production brief

**Reference status:** five visually checked product images in [`sources.json`](/Users/vladimir/Downloads/RAID_GAME_Art_Overhaul_References/apparel/gloves_olive/sources.json) show several olive tactical glove constructions, including palm/back pairs, finger pads, cuff closures and knuckle protection variants. Treat the set as a family study rather than a single uniform model. All source images are reference-only.

## Silhouette and paneling

Build a full-finger olive field glove with clear individual finger sleeves, separate thumb gusset, low-profile reinforced palm, flexible dorsal fabric, and a compact padded or molded knuckle zone where the chosen variant brief calls for it. Use a short wrist cuff with a low-profile closure. Do not combine all visible product-specific features into one glove; choose one coherent existing variant and preserve its readable panel layout.

## Materials, UV and movement

Use matte olive textile on the back and fingers, with darker slightly grippier palm and modest leather-like reinforcements. Keep stitching and panel transitions visible at close range, but avoid oversized seams or high gloss. Align textile grain along fingers and cuff; avoid mirrored identical marks across the pair. Keep each finger distinct through curl, grip, reload and open-hand poses. References show product views, not this game's held-weapon contact: verify grip, trigger reach and cuff/sleeve overlap in runtime.
