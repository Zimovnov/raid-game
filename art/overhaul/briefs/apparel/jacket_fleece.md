# jacket_fleece — production brief

**Reference status:** five visually reviewed fleece-layer product photos in [`sources.json`](/Users/vladimir/Downloads/RAID_GAME_Art_Overhaul_References/apparel/jacket_fleece/sources.json) show front and three-quarter silhouettes, high collars, zip closures, pocket placement, cuffs and variations in pile/soft-shell reinforcement. They are different products, so use common construction cues rather than joining every feature into one jacket. Rear-panel details are not consistently visible and should stay simple. Images are reference-only.

## Silhouette and construction

Create a mid-layer jacket with a waist-length/hip-length straight body, front zipper, high soft collar, long sleeves, elastic or bound cuffs and a simple hem. Use either low-profile hand pockets or one chest/arm pocket pair according to the selected catalog variant. Reinforced shoulder/elbow panels are optional only where the chosen design calls for them. Do not add a hood unless selected by a specific item variant.

## Material and fit

Use short-pile matte fleece with a fine, low-contrast surface; avoid long wool fibers or plastic shine. Optional woven reinforcement panels use a flatter, tighter fabric response. Keep seams and pocket edges readable without chunky raised piping. Test arm raise, elbow bend, torso twist and crouch so the soft layer compresses naturally under outerwear and gear. UV scale should remain stable on collar, sleeves, cuffs and body; LOD0/LOD1 preserve pile shading as material response rather than geometric fuzz.

## Limits

References do not resolve a universal back-panel, hood or pocket layout; choose one coherent model variant and use a simple back with no invented pockets. Exclude logos, labels and exact brand details.
