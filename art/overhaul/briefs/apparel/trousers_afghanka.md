# trousers_afghanka — production brief

**Reference status:** five visually checked images in [`sources.json`](/Users/vladimir/Downloads/RAID_GAME_Art_Overhaul_References/apparel/trousers_afghanka/sources.json) include a worn profile, a historical uniform context, two clear product views and a rear view. The historical photo is context only; garment construction follows the clearer individual product images. All images are reference-only.

## Silhouette and construction

Create straight, roomy field trousers with a high waistband, broad seat and thighs, simple side pockets and a utilitarian straight leg. Keep the cut comparatively plain, avoiding later cargo-pocket or knee-pad features that are not visible. Allow a soft break above the boot and modest gathers at the waist/seat/knee. Do not add hidden devices or exact fastener mechanisms that the refs do not resolve.

## Material, UV and movement

Use matte khaki cotton twill with visible folds, moderate fading and wear at the knees/hem; material must read as heavier than a thin summer oversuit but softer than armor. Keep UV scale consistent across front/back and both legs, with no obvious mirrored abrasion. Test crouch, kneel, sprint and foot lift against the pelvis/thigh/shin joints so crotch and knee folds remain natural and the hem does not intersect footwear. LOD0/LOD1 preserve the broad leg silhouette and waist.

## Limits

The photos mix historical context and commercial garments. They establish only a generic Afghanka-like straight trouser silhouette. No insignia, source labels, logos or third-party textures may enter the game.
