# head_bandana — production brief

**Reference status:** five visually checked images in [`sources.json`](/Users/vladimir/Downloads/RAID_GAME_Art_Overhaul_References/apparel/head_bandana/sources.json) show front, side, rear knot/tails, ear coverage and alternate fabric examples. `01` and `02` are related views of one construction and are counted as complementary angles, not separate designs. Printed examples are construction references only; no print or branding may be copied. Sources are reference-only.

## Silhouette and construction

Use a lightweight triangular or multi-panel headwrap that covers the crown and ties at the back, with a compact knot and two short fabric tails. The front edge follows the hairline; side panels cover the upper ear where shown but do not form a rigid cap. Keep the tails distinct from the collar and avoid excessive cloth volume.

## Material and UV

Use matte thin cotton or a lightweight woven fabric, with one plain olive variant or the selected matching camouflage material. Unwrap crown, side panels, tie and tails as coherent cloth zones; keep print scale consistent with the associated uniform and avoid a seam running through a conspicuous repeated patch. The cloth should drape softly with slight folds, not look like molded rubber.

## Fit and movement

Anchor the knot/tails at the rear head area, follow the existing head joint and keep ears, eyes and face readable. Check head turns, crouch and sprint transitions; tails should move subtly, never intersect the collar or become rigid spikes. Use a generic knot/closure because source construction varies; do not reproduce brand labels or national markings.
