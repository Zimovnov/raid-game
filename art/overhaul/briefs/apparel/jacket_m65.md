# jacket_m65 — production brief

**Reference status:** five visually reviewed product/gallery images in [`sources.json`](/Users/vladimir/Downloads/RAID_GAME_Art_Overhaul_References/apparel/jacket_m65/sources.json) show the front silhouette, alternate closure state, full-panel variations, and close views of collar/cuffs/pockets. Some files are composites of details rather than separate angles; they are counted for their distinct construction information, not as additional silhouettes. The source set establishes an M65-style field jacket family, not runtime rights or an exact specification.

## Silhouette and construction

Build a hip-length field jacket with a roomy straight body, stand/fold collar, central covered closure, four large bellows-style front pockets with flaps, adjustable cuffs and an adjustable waist/hem. Include the visible shoulder and elbow shaping without adding armor. The back should remain a simple panel with a restrained seam/yoke; do not add unshown back pockets. Hood and liner are separate variants only if already supported by the garment catalog.

## Material and motion

Use matte, moderately heavy cotton/nylon woven fabric in olive or the selected color variant, with restrained wear on high points. Pocket flaps, cuffs and closure use separate UV islands aligned to cloth grain; avoid mirrored asymmetry and stretched pocket texture. Test arm raise, elbow bend, torso turn and crouch so the jacket hem and pockets follow the body without clipping or reading as rigid boxes. LOD0/LOD1 keep collar, four-pocket layout and cuff silhouette legible.

## Provenance limits

The commercial photos are reference-only. Do not transfer logos, labels, branded zipper details or image pixels into runtime. Use the references for broad field-jacket construction and create an original game garment.
