# trousers_kombat — production brief

**Reference status:** five SSO product-gallery images were inspected individually; see [`sources.json`](/Users/vladimir/Downloads/RAID_GAME_Art_Overhaul_References/apparel/trousers_kombat/sources.json). The set contains multiple full trouser views, an angled rear view and a labeled construction/detail plate. It supports cargo-pocket placement, knee reinforcement, adjustment areas and fabric transitions. These are design references only; do not reproduce brand marks or copy the exact camouflage.

## Silhouette and construction

Use a moderately loose combat-trouser cut with waistband and belt loops, articulated leg panels, large thigh/cargo pockets, compact utility pockets, reinforced knees with adjustable-height knee zones, and a practical tapered lower leg with adjustment. Preserve the visible distinction between soft body cloth and reinforced knee/cuff zones. The reference product's exact fasteners and proprietary textiles are not required; do not invent hidden mechanisms from annotated marketing callouts.

## Material and animation

Use matte woven/ripstop fabric; make reinforcement zones slightly heavier and more abrasion-resistant in response without looking like rigid armor. Keep any camouflage as a separate fabric variant and orient/offset UVs per panel without mirrored repeating clusters. Fit waist/pelvis, thighs, shins and feet to the existing 13-joint anchors. Check standing, crouch, kneel, sprint and knee bend: cargo pockets and knee areas must follow deformation without clipping or turning into rigid boxes. Preserve a readable pocket/knee silhouette in LOD0 and LOD1.
